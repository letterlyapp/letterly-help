---
question: "Is there a recording time limit?"
order: 2
---

Yes, and it depends on your plan: up to **3 minutes** per recording on Free and up to **90 minutes** on Pro. When a recording reaches its limit, Letterly stops and saves it automatically — nothing is lost.
