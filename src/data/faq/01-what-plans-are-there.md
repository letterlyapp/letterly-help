---
question: "What plans are there?"
order: 1
---

Letterly has a **Free** plan and a **Pro** plan. Free gives you 10 notes, recordings up to 3 minutes and 20 dictations. Pro removes these limits and comes as a monthly, annual or lifetime plan.
