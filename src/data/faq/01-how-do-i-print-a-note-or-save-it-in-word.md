---
question: "How do I print a note or save it in Word?"
order: 1
---

Letterly does not print or save Word files itself. Open the note and tap the copy button, then paste the text into Word, Pages, Google Docs or any other app you like — and print it from there. You can also use **⋯ → Share with** to send the note straight into that app.
