The **Rewrite Options Gallery** is a library of ready-made rewrite options. Browse it to find the ones that match your style, and add only those to your Rewrite panel. Read about the [Rewrite options panel](/rewrite-options-panel/) here.

<Callout type="note">

Adding options from the gallery is available on **Pro**. On the Free plan you have the **Magic** option.

<MoreLink href="/what-plans-are-there/">Learn more about plans →</MoreLink>

</Callout>

## Open the gallery

<Steps>

1. Open any note and tap **Rewrite**.
2. Tap **+ New**.
3. Tap **Choose from gallery**.
4. Tap **Add** on the options you want to use.

</Steps>

<Video src="/images/help-center/open-the-gallery.mp4" caption="Opening the Rewrite Options Gallery" />

After you add an option, you can:

- Tap **Customize** to edit it before using it.
- Tap **Apply now** to use it on the current note right away.

Added options also appear in your Rewrite panel immediately.

<Callout type="tip">

Deleted an option you need again? Add it back from the gallery — the default options stay available there.

</Callout>
