## What is the Letterly widget?

The Letterly widget is a small shortcut you place on your screen to **start a new recording with one tap**. On iPhone it works on the Home Screen and the Lock Screen.

## Before you start

- The **Letterly app** is installed on your device.
- Your iOS version supports widgets.

<Callout type="tip">

Do not see Letterly in the widget list? Make sure the app is updated to the latest version, and that you have opened it and logged in at least once.

</Callout>

## Add the widget to your Home Screen

<Steps>

1. Touch and hold an empty area on your Home Screen until the icons start to jiggle.
2. Tap **Edit** in the top-left corner, then tap **Add Widget**.
3. Search for **Letterly** in the widget gallery.
4. Tap **Add Widget**, then drag it where you want.
5. Tap **Done** to save your layout.

</Steps>

<Shot />

## Add the widget to your Lock Screen

<Steps>

1. Go to your Lock Screen and touch and hold it until **Customize** appears.
2. Tap **Customize** and choose your Lock Screen.
3. Scroll or search for **Letterly** in the list.
4. Tap the Letterly widget to add it.
5. Tap **Done** to save your Lock Screen.

</Steps>

<Shot />

## Start a recording from the widget

<Steps>

1. Tap the Letterly widget.
2. Letterly opens directly to a new recording, so you can start speaking right away.
3. Tap **Stop** when you are done.
4. Letterly processes the note, and the transcript and rewrites appear in the app.

</Steps>

<Platform name="android">

## What is the Letterly widget?

The Letterly widget is a small shortcut you place on your Home Screen to **start a new recording with one tap**.

## Add the widget

<Steps>

1. Touch and hold an empty space on your Home Screen.
2. Tap **Widgets** in the menu that appears.
3. Scroll the list or use search to find **Letterly**.
4. Touch and hold the Letterly widget, then drag it onto your Home Screen.
5. Drop it where you want it to live.

</Steps>

You can move it later the same way: touch and hold, then drag.

<Shot />

<Callout type="tip">

Place the widget near the bottom of the screen, or where your thumb naturally rests, so you can start recording with one hand.

</Callout>

## Start a recording from the widget

<Steps>

1. Tap the Letterly widget on your Home Screen.
2. Letterly opens to a new recording screen.
3. Speak your note, then tap **Stop**.
4. Your note is saved and processed in the app, just like any other recording.

</Steps>
