When a subscription is canceled or expires, you keep Pro until the end of the period you already paid for. After that your account moves to the **Free** plan.

## What happens to your notes

<Callout type="note">

Nothing is deleted. All your existing notes, versions and recordings stay in your account, and you can still read, search and export them.

</Callout>

On the Free plan, new notes are limited again — up to **10 notes**, each recording up to **3 minutes**, with the **Magic** rewrite option. See [What plans are there?](/what-plans-are-there/) for the full comparison.

Subscribe again at any time and everything is unlocked immediately.
