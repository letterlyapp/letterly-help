The **Dictation Bubble** is a floating button that stays on top of other apps. Tap it to dictate in any app on your phone without opening Letterly.

<Callout type="note">

On the **Free** plan: **20 dictations**, each up to **1 minute**.

<MoreLink href="/what-plans-are-there/">Learn more about plans →</MoreLink>

</Callout>

Before you start, add the Bubble to your device — see [Set up Dictation](/set-up-dictation/).

<Callout type="tip">

The Bubble works in most apps with a text field, including Messages, Gmail, WhatsApp, Notes, and ChatGPT.

</Callout>

## Dictate with the Bubble

<Figure src="/images/blog/how-to-use-dictation-bubble-on-android-6.webp" alt="Tap a text field to place your cursor" caption="Tap a text field to place your cursor" />

<Steps>

1. Open the app you want to write in and tap a text field to place the cursor.
2. Tap the **Dictation Bubble**. Recording starts immediately.
3. Say what you want to write.
4. Tap the **Stop** button. Letterly processes your speech and inserts the text at the cursor.

</Steps>

<Figure src="/images/blog/how-to-use-dictation-bubble-on-android-7.webp" alt="Recording controls on the Dictation Bubble" caption="Recording controls on the Dictation Bubble" />

| Control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **X** | Discards the current recording |
| **Stop** (red button) | Ends the recording and inserts the text |

<Callout type="warn">

Keep the app and the text field open while Letterly processes your dictation. If the field closes, the text cannot be inserted.

</Callout>

If the audio bars are moving, Letterly is recording. If they stay still while you speak, check that Letterly has permission to use the microphone.

## Move the Bubble

Press and hold the Bubble and drag it to any position on the screen.

## Snooze the Bubble

To hide the Bubble temporarily, drag it to the **Snooze** area at the bottom of the screen. The Bubble disappears for 10 minutes and then returns automatically.

To bring it back sooner, unlock your phone and shake it.

## If the text was not inserted

Letterly saves every completed dictation. If you started dictating without placing the cursor in a text field, open **Settings → History** to find and copy the text.
