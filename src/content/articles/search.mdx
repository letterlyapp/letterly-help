Search finds notes by the words they contain — in the transcript, in rewritten versions, and in titles.

## Find a note

<Steps>

1. On the notes screen, tap the **search** icon.
2. Type any part of a word.

</Steps>

The list filters as you type. You do not need the whole word — typing *pres* finds *presentation*.

<Callout type="warn">

Search matches the words you type, not their meaning. Synonyms and related terms are not found, so use the wording you remember from the note.

</Callout>
