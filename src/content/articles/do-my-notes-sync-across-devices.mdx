Yes. Notes sync automatically across every device where you are signed in with the same email — start a recording on your phone and pick it up on your computer moments later.

## What syncs across devices

- **Notes, transcripts, and rewritten versions**
- **Tags and titles**
- **Audio recordings**

<Callout type="note">

Dictation History is stored locally and does not sync across your devices.

<MoreLink href="/history/">Learn more about Dictation History →</MoreLink>

</Callout>

<Callout type="warn">

Signed in with a different email on another device? You will see a different, empty set of notes. Check the email in **Settings → Letterly Account**.

</Callout>

<Callout type="note">

Using Letterly without an account? Your notes stay on that device only. Sign in to start syncing.

<MoreLink href="/onboarding/">Learn more about Onboarding →</MoreLink>

</Callout>
