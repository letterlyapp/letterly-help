The **Rewrite options panel** is your list of rewrite options. Letterly comes with a set of common ones, and you can shape the list so it only shows what you actually use.

<Callout type="note">

Adding options to your panel is available on **Pro**. On the Free plan you have the **Magic** option.

<MoreLink href="/what-plans-are-there/">Learn more about plans →</MoreLink>

</Callout>

## Open the panel

You can open it in two ways:

- **From Settings** — tap the cogwheel in the top right, then **General → Rewrite options**.
- **From a note** — open any note and tap **Rewrite**.

## Manage your options

<Figure src="/images/help-center/rewrite-panel-actions.png" alt="1 — Copy, 2 — Edit, 3 — Delete" caption="1 — Copy, 2 — Edit, 3 — Delete" />

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **1. Copy** | Duplicates an option so you can tweak the copy and keep the original |
| **2. Edit** | Changes an existing option — adjust the **Quick settings**, or tap **Advanced settings** to write your own instructions in the **Prompt** field |
| **3. Delete** | Removes an option from the panel |

<Callout type="tip">

Delete the options you never use, so the panel stays short and the ones you need are easy to find.

</Callout>
