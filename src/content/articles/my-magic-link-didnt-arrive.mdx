Check your **Spam** or **Promotions** folder first — the email sometimes lands there, and it can take a couple of minutes to arrive.

Double-check that you typed your email address correctly, then request a new email.

<Callout type="note">

On iPhone, the same email also contains a **6-digit code**. If the link does not work in your mail app, tap **Enter code manually** on the **Check your email** screen and type the code instead.

</Callout>

Still nothing? Try **Continue with Google** on the login screen, or email us at [hi@letterly.app](mailto:hi@letterly.app) and we'll check what happened.
