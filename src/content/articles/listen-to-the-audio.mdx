<PlatformTabs>
<Platform name="iphone">

Every recording keeps its original audio. Open the note and use the **audio player** at the top to listen back to what you recorded.

## Play a recording

<Steps>

1. Open the note.
2. Tap the **play** button next to the date.

</Steps>

<Figure src="/images/help-center/audio-player-iphone.png" alt="The audio player, with the speed control" caption="The audio player, with the speed control" />

<Callout type="note">

Playback continues when you leave the note, so you can listen while you look through your other notes.

</Callout>

## Change the speed

Tap the speed control in the player to switch between **1×**, **1.5×**, and **2×**.

## Notes with several recordings

If you used **Record more**, each session is stored separately. The player lists them one after another, so you can choose which part to play and switch between them. See [Record more](/record-more/).

</Platform>
