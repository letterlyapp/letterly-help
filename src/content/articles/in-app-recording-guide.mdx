Hey! We designed Letterly to be intuitive, so you probably don't need a guide. But if you like to explore and don't want to miss anything, here's a quick tour. 😊

<Eyebrow>Part 1</Eyebrow>

## Four steps to your first note

### Step 1. Download Letterly

Get Letterly for iPhone from the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/letterly-voice-to-clear-text/id6464049772).

<Callout type="tip" title="Good to know:">

- One subscription covers all your devices — phone, computer, tablet.
- Notes sync automatically — start on your phone, continue on your computer.
- Up to 90 minutes per note, with unlimited notes and total recording time.

</Callout>

### Step 2. Log in

<Figure src="/images/blog/your-guide-to-the-letterly-mobile-app-2.webp" alt="Login screens" />

Open the app, tap **I have an account** and choose a method. **Log in with the same email you used when you purchased the app.**

- **Google** — tap Continue a couple of times.
- **Apple** — when asked to Share or Hide your email, choose **Share**.
- **Email** — tap **Continue with Email**. We send a magic link and a 6-digit code to your inbox, and you can use either one.

With **Continue with Email** you have two ways in:

- **Use the magic link** — tap **Sign in to Letterly** in the email.
- **Enter the code manually** — on the **Check your email** screen, tap **Enter code manually** and type the 6-digit code.

<Callout type="warn" title="If you sign in with email, keep in mind:">

- The link and the code are valid for **15 minutes**, so finish the login right away.
- Each of them can be used **only once**. If you have already used one, request a new one in the app.
- Open the link or enter the code on the **same device** you started the login from.

</Callout>

### Step 3. Record your first note

<Figure src="/images/blog/your-guide-to-the-letterly-mobile-app-3.webp" alt="Recording screen" />

Tap the **microphone button** in the middle and start talking — don't worry about pauses or “ums”. Tap **Stop** when you're done.

<Callout type="tip">

If the dots aren't moving, your microphone may be off — check the app's microphone permission.

</Callout>

### Step 4. Improve your text

<Figure src="/images/blog/your-guide-to-the-letterly-mobile-app-4.webp" alt="Rewrite screen" />

After Stop, Letterly transcribes your voice and opens the note. Tap the big **Rewrite** button and pick an option — e.g. **Significantly** fully rewrites it, **Facebook Post** turns it into a post. Wait a few seconds for the polished version.

- Swipe left / right to switch between the original and your rewritten versions.
- Tap the **star** next to an option to pin it to the top for next time.

Want more options? Tap **+ New**:

<Figure src="/images/blog/your-guide-to-the-letterly-mobile-app-9.webp" alt="Create from scratch" />

**Create from scratch** — a fully custom rewrite option, e.g. “Rewrite as if written by a lawyer”, saved for future use.

<Figure src="/images/blog/your-guide-to-the-letterly-mobile-app-10.webp" alt="Rewrite Options Gallery" />

**Choose from gallery** — the Rewrite Options Gallery; tap **Add** on any option to add it to your list.

## Four screens you need to know

### Screen 1. Before recording

<Figure src="/images/blog/your-guide-to-the-letterly-mobile-app-5.webp" alt="Notes screen" />

1. **Sort** notes by last update or creation date.
2. **Group by tags** (e.g. Content, Clients, Personal).
3. **Add audio or text note** — type a note, or upload an audio file to transcribe.
4. **Tap to record** a new note.
5. **Search** notes by keyword.
6. **All your notes** live here.
7. **Settings** — account and preferences.

### Screen 2. While recording

<Figure src="/images/blog/your-guide-to-the-letterly-mobile-app-6.webp" alt="Recording controls" />

1. **Back** — returns to the previous screen (the note won't be saved).
2. **Stop** — ends the recording.
3. **Restart** — starts over (what you recorded won't be saved).
4. **Pause** — pause to think, tap again to resume.
5. **Multi-speaker mode** — separates voices by speaker for interviews and meetings.

### Screen 3. After recording

<Figure src="/images/blog/your-guide-to-the-letterly-mobile-app-7.webp" alt="Note screen" />

1. **Audio playback** — listen to your original recording.
2. **Auto-generated title.**
3. **Transcript** — the full text.
4. **Tags** — none, one, or many.
5. **Copy** — use your note anywhere.
6. **Rewrite with AI.**
7. **Actions** — connect to Google Docs, Notion, etc. via Zapier / webhooks.
8. **Note settings (⋯):** Record more · Translate · Share with · Export audio · Speaker's language · Add / remove speaker roles · Archive · Text replacements.

### Screen 4. Account settings

<Figure src="/images/blog/your-guide-to-the-letterly-mobile-app-8.webp" alt="Settings screen" />

Tap the cogwheel in the top-right corner:

1. See app **updates**.
2. Set the **speaker's language** (applies to future notes).
3. Manage **rewrite options** — pin, search, or create.
4. Manage all your **tags**.
5. Set up **integrations** (Zapier / webhooks).
6. Set up **Text Replacements**.
7. Connect Letterly to **AI tools** (Claude, ChatGPT).
8. Light / Dark / System **mode**.
9. Set the **interface language**.
10. **Current plan** — plan, renewal date, and where it's managed.
11. **Letterly Account** — the email you're logged in with (should show **Letterly Pro** if it's correct).

You did it! 🎉 Run into something? See [Troubleshooting](/my-magic-link-didnt-arrive/).
