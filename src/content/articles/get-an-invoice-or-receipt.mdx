Your receipt and invoice are sent to your email automatically after the purchase. Check your **Spam** folder if you cannot find them.

Need a custom invoice — company name, VAT number, a specific format? Write to [hi@letterly.app](mailto:hi@letterly.app) and we will prepare it.

## Bought in the App Store or Google Play

Apple and Google issue those documents themselves, so you need to request them from your Apple ID or Google Play account.
