Record a note straight from your wrist. If Letterly is installed on your iPhone, it appears on your Apple Watch automatically.

<Callout type="note">

Letterly on Apple Watch is available on **Pro**.

<MoreLink href="/what-plans-are-there/">Learn more about plans →</MoreLink>

</Callout>

## Record with Letterly on Apple Watch

<Steps>

1. Open Letterly on your Apple Watch.
2. Tap to start recording.
3. Tap stop when you are done.
4. Wait for the **Synced** status.

</Steps>

<Callout type="warn">

Make sure the note shows **Synced** before you close the app. Otherwise it may not appear on your iPhone or Mac.

</Callout>

Apple Watch is for quick recording only. You cannot view the transcript there — open Letterly on your phone or computer to review, rewrite, or work with your notes.

## Multi-speaker mode on Apple Watch

You can switch between multi-speaker and single-speaker recording before or during a recording.

<Steps>

1. Open Letterly on your Apple Watch.
2. On the recording screen, tap the speaker mode button.
3. Choose multi-speaker on if more than one person is speaking, or off if only you are.

</Steps>

## Add Letterly to a watch face

<Steps>

1. Open the Apple Watch app on your iPhone.
2. Tap **My Watch**.
3. Select a watch face from **My Faces** — Modular, Modular Compact, Activity Digital, Nike Compact, or Nike Digital.
4. Tap one of the complication areas.
5. Choose **Letterly** if it is available for that slot.

</Steps>
