## Rewrite text from the keyboard

The **Rewrite** button appears in the Letterly Keyboard when Letterly has just inserted your dictated text, or when you select existing text.

Tap **Rewrite** to choose an option such as **Magic**, **List**, **Structured**, or **Journal Entry**.

## Choose a rewrite option

<Figure src="/images/blog/how-to-use-letterly-keyboard-6.webp" alt="The rewrite options menu in the keyboard" caption="The rewrite options menu in the keyboard" />

| Section | What it contains |
| --- | --- |
| **Rewrite by voice** | Say how you want the text changed |
| **Custom prompts** | Rewrite options you created yourself |
| **General options** | Ready-made rewrite options from Letterly |

Your favourite rewrite options appear at the top of the menu. Choose which ones are shown there in the Letterly app — see [Favorite rewrite options](/favorite-rewrite-options/).

## Rewrite by voice

Instead of choosing a ready-made option, describe what you want done with the text. Any request works, for example:

- make it shorter
- translate to English
- make the tone friendlier
- turn this into bullet points

There are two ways to use it:

| What you select | What Letterly rewrites |
| --- | --- |
| A fragment of text | The selected fragment |
| Nothing | Your last dictated phrase |

<Callout type="tip">

To undo the result, tap **Undo** in the keyboard menu.

</Callout>

## If the Rewrite button disappears

The Rewrite button disappears after you change the text, for example by typing something new or editing a word. To rewrite the text again, select it first.

<Platform name="mac">

Letterly does not only transcribe what you said. When you finish dictating, it cleans up or rewrites your text automatically.

There are two types of rewrite options:

| Type | When it applies |
| --- | --- |
| **Main rewrite option** | Automatically, after every dictation |
| **Pinned rewrite options** | When you choose one from the floating bar |

## Main rewrite option

Your Main rewrite option is applied every time you stop dictating.

By default, Letterly uses the **System** rewrite option, created specifically for Dictation. It keeps the text close to what you said, while removing filler words, adding punctuation, and making the text easier to read.

You can choose any rewrite option as your main one, including **Structured**, **List**, **Short Summary**, and **Journal Entry**.

<Callout type="note">

The Main rewrite option cannot be turned off. To get your dictated text without any changes, choose **Raw Transcript**.

</Callout>

### Change the Main rewrite option

Open **Settings → Dictation → Floating Bar → Dictation Output**, then choose your Main rewrite option.

<Shot label="Screenshot: Dictation Output settings on Mac" />

## Pinned rewrite options

Pinned rewrite options appear below the recording controls on the floating bar. Use them to change the format of your dictation right after you finish. Each option has a keyboard shortcut next to it.

<Figure src="/images/help-center/mac-floating-bar-controls.png" alt="Pinned rewrite options on the floating bar" />

### Change pinned rewrite options

Open **Settings → Dictation → Floating Bar → Dictation Output**, then click the pin icon next to each option you want to keep on the floating bar.

<Callout type="tip">

Pinned options are optional. You can remove all of them.

</Callout>
