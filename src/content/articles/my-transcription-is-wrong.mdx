Start with how much of the text is wrong.

## The whole text is wrong

- **Listen to the recording** and check that your voice came through clearly, without heavy noise or drop-outs.
- **Set the speaker's language** manually in the note settings. Letterly will transcribe the audio again.

## Individual words are wrong

If Letterly keeps misspelling a name, a brand, or a technical term, add it to your [Dictionary](/dictionary/) — Letterly will use your spelling in future notes.

## The word is in the Dictionary and still comes out wrong

- Check that it is in the list and spelled exactly as you want to see it.
- Check that you added it **before** the recording — the Dictionary is applied while the note is transcribed, so it does not change older notes.
- Say the word clearly, with a small pause around it.

<Callout type="note">

Need to replace one word with another — an abbreviation, an address, a standard phrase? That is a text replacement, not a dictionary word.

</Callout>
