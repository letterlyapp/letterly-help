The Bubble needs the **Display over other apps** permission to stay on your screen. If Android or a cleaner app switched it off, the Bubble stops appearing.

<Steps>

1. Open Letterly and check that the **Dictation Bubble** is turned on in **Settings → Dictation**.
2. Check the permission in Android **Settings → Apps → Letterly → Display over other apps**.
3. Turn off **battery optimization** for Letterly, so Android does not stop it in the background.
4. Restart the phone if the Bubble still does not come back.

</Steps>

<Callout type="note">

The Bubble is also hidden by design in some places — for example on the lock screen and in apps that block overlays, such as banking apps.

</Callout>
