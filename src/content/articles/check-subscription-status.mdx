Open **Settings → Current plan**. You will see your plan, the renewal date, and where the subscription is managed.

Just below, **Letterly Account** shows the email you are signed in with.

<Callout type="warn">

Your plan only appears if you are signed in with the **same email you used for the purchase**. If it still says Free, see [I paid but my account shows Free](/i-paid-but-my-account-shows-free/).

</Callout>

<Callout type="note" title="Bought on our website?">

Manage everything in the subscription portal. Sign in with the **same email you used for the purchase** — otherwise the portal will not find your subscription.

</Callout>
