Notes sync automatically when you are signed in with the same email everywhere. If a note is missing on one device:

<Steps>

1. Open **Settings → Letterly Account** on both devices and check that the **email is the same**.
2. Check the internet connection on the device where you recorded the note.
3. **Keep the app open** for a minute so it can finish syncing — a note only syncs after it has been processed.
4. Pull down the notes list to refresh it, then fully close and reopen the app.

</Steps>

<Callout type="warn">

Do not delete the app on the device where the note was recorded until it has synced.

</Callout>

Still missing? Write to [hi@letterly.app](mailto:hi@letterly.app) and tell us when you recorded the note and on which device.

<Callout type="note">

If you are on the **Free** plan and have reached **10 notes**, a newer note stays on the device where you made it and does not sync.
