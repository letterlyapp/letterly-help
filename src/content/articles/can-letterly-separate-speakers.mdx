Yes. Letterly can split a transcript by who said what, so an interview or a meeting reads as a dialogue instead of one block of text.

## Turn it on

**During a recording** — tap the **Multi-speaker** button on the recording screen. The transcript is split by speaker as soon as it is ready.

**After a recording** — open the note, tap the **⋯** menu, and choose **Add speaker roles**. Letterly processes the audio again and splits the transcript by speaker.

<Callout type="tip">

To split a note that was recorded without Multi-speaker, open the note, tap **⋯**, and choose **Add speaker roles**.

</Callout>

## What the transcript looks like

Each turn in the conversation gets its own label — **Speaker 1**, **Speaker 2**, and so on. Letterly separates the voices, but it does not know who the people are, so the labels stay generic.

## Get better separation

- Place the device where it can hear everyone, not next to one person.
- Ask people not to talk over each other — overlapping speech is the hardest case to split.
- Avoid heavy background noise: cafés, air conditioning, speakerphone echo.

<Callout type="warn">

Voices that sound very similar, or moments when several people speak at once, may end up under the same label.

</Callout>
