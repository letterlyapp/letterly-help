Merging combines several existing notes into one.

## To merge notes:

<Steps>

1. On the notes screen, press and hold a note to select it, then select the others.
2. Choose **Merge**.

</Steps>

<Video src="/images/help-center/merge-notes.mp4" caption="Merging notes" />

Letterly combines them into a single note in the order you selected.

<Callout type="note">

The original notes are automatically moved to Archive. Tap or click **Undo merge** to restore them.

</Callout>

<Callout type="tip">

Merging works on notes you already have. To add new audio to an existing note, use [Record more](/record-more/).

</Callout>
