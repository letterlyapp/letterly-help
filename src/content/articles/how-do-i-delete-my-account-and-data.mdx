Deleting your account removes your notes and recordings from our servers.

## Delete your account

<Steps>

1. Open **Settings → Account**.
2. Choose **Delete account**.
3. Fill in the deletion request and send it.

</Steps>

We process deletion requests within **72 hours**.

<Callout type="warn">

Deletion is permanent. Your notes, recordings, and rewritten versions cannot be restored afterwards.

</Callout>

<Callout type="tip">

Want to keep something? Export it first — see [Export notes](/export-notes/) and [Download audio](/download-audio/).

</Callout>

Need help, or cannot reach the setting? Email [hi@letterly.app](mailto:hi@letterly.app) and we will take care of it.
