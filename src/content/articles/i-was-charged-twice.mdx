In most cases this is not a double charge. Two common reasons:

- You **upgraded to Lifetime** — you paid for your first plan, then the remaining difference, so the two amounts add up to the Lifetime price.
- A payment looked like it failed and was retried.

<Callout type="warn">

If you bought a second plan without cancelling the first one, you may have **two active subscriptions**. See [Change my plan](/change-my-plan/).

</Callout>

If you really were charged twice, write to [hi@letterly.app](mailto:hi@letterly.app) with the details — the dates, amounts and the email you paid with — and we will refund the extra charge.
