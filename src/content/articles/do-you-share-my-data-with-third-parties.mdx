No. We never sell your data and never share it for anyone else's purposes — our business is selling software, not information.

We use third-party providers to transcribe your recordings and rewrite your text. They process the data only to return that result, and **do not use it to train their models**. The current list is in our [Privacy Policy](https://letterly.app/policy/#whoshare).
