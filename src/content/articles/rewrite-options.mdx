A **rewrite option** turns your transcript into the text you actually need — a clean note, a message, a summary, a post. The transcript stays as it is; every rewrite is saved as a separate version of the note.

<Callout type="note">

On the **Free** plan you have the **Magic** option and **10 rewrites** in total. Every other option is marked **Pro**.

<MoreLink href="/what-plans-are-there/">Learn more about plans →</MoreLink>

</Callout>

## Rewrite a note

<Steps>

1. Open the note and tap **Rewrite**.
2. Choose an option, for example **Magic**, **Structured**, or **Short Summary**.
3. Wait a few seconds for the new version to appear.

</Steps>

<Video src="/images/help-center/rewrite-a-note.mp4" caption="Rewriting a note" />

<Callout type="note">

You can leave the note or close the app while a rewrite is running. Letterly finishes it in the background.

</Callout>

## Which option to choose

| Option | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Raw Transcript** | Your words exactly as spoken |
| **Slightly** | Tidies up the text, keeps your wording and tone |
| **Magic** | Cleans up rough thoughts into clear, readable text |
| **Significantly** | Fully reshapes the text into a new structure |
| **Format options** | Turn the text into a specific shape — **List**, **Structured**, **Email**, **Journal Entry**, **Meeting Takeaways**, and more |

## Your list of options

The Rewrite panel shows the options you use, not everything Letterly has.

<Figure src="/images/help-center/rewrite-open-panel.png" alt="Tap Rewrite to open your list of options" caption="Tap Rewrite to open your list of options" />

- **Favorites** appear at the top of the list. See [Favorite rewrite options](/favorite-rewrite-options/).
- **Custom rewrite options** can be created from scratch or copied from an existing one. See [Custom rewrite options](/custom-rewrite-options/).
- **More options** can be added from the [Rewrite options gallery](/rewrite-options-gallery/).

## Switch between versions

Swipe left and right to move between versions, or tap the version name at the top of the note to pick one from the list.

<Video src="/images/help-center/switch-versions.mp4" caption="Switching between versions of a note" />

Version names show the path the text took — for example **Magic / Italian** means the text was rewritten, then translated.

## Rewrite a rewritten text

You can rewrite either the **Original transcript** or an already **rewritten version**.

Rewrite options can be applied one after another. For example, you can first make a note shorter, then turn that shortened version into a list.

There are two ways to rewrite an existing version:

<Steps>

1. **Swipe to the version** you want to rewrite, then tap **Rewrite** and choose another option.
2. Tap **Rewrite**, then use the arrow at the top to choose which version you want to rewrite.

</Steps>
