No. Your recordings, notes and rewritten text are never used to train AI models — neither ours nor our providers'.

Letterly sends your audio and text to third-party providers only so they can transcribe or rewrite it and return the result. Details are in our [Privacy Policy](https://letterly.app/policy/).
