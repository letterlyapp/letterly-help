<Platform name="iphone">

## Download the audio

<Steps>

1. Open the note and tap the **⋯** menu.
2. Choose **Export audio**.
3. Save the file, or send it to another app.

</Steps>

<Callout type="note">

You can download a recording from any device where you are signed in, not only the one you recorded on.

</Callout>

<Callout type="tip">

Looking to send the note to someone rather than save a file? See [Share](/share/).

</Callout>

</Platform>
