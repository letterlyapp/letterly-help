No. iOS and Android do not give apps access to call audio, so no app — including Letterly — can record a phone call directly.

The workaround:

<Steps>

1. Put the call on **speakerphone**.
2. Record it with Letterly on a second device placed nearby.

</Steps>

<Callout type="note" title="Ask before you record.">

Tell the other participants that you are recording and wait for their consent.

</Callout>
