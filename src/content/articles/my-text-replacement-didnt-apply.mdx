A text replacement swaps a phrase you say for the text you want to see. If nothing was replaced:

- **Check the trigger.** Open **Settings → Text replacements** and compare it with what you actually said — it has to match.
- **Say it clearly and separately**, with a small pause around it.
- **It applies to new notes.** Replacements added afterwards do not change notes you already recorded.

<Callout type="note">

If you only need the correct spelling of a name or a term, add it to the Dictionary instead.
