After the intro screens during [Onboarding](/onboarding/), Letterly shows the paywall — the screen where you choose a plan.

<Steps>

1. **Choose a plan** that fits you. Not sure which? See [What plans are there?](/what-plans-are-there/)
2. **Confirm payment.** Payment goes through your app store or card, depending on where you're subscribing — details in [Change payment method](/change-payment-method/).
3. **You're in.** After paying you land on the main screen, ready to make your [first note](/what-is-a-note/).

</Steps>

<Callout type="tip" title="Not ready to pay?">

Tap the **✕** in the corner of the paywall to keep using Letterly on the **Free** plan — up to **10 notes**, each recording up to **3 minutes**. You can upgrade anytime.

</Callout>
