<Steps>

1. Open the note's settings and **download the audio file** (so it's saved locally, just in case).
2. **Fully close** the app — swipe it away — then reopen it.
3. **Keep the app open** for a couple of minutes so it can sync.

</Steps>

If the note is still stuck, email [hi@letterly.app](mailto:hi@letterly.app) and we'll help recover it.
