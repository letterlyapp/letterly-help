You can buy Pro in three places. Where you bought it decides **where your subscription is managed** afterwards — so it is worth knowing which one you used.

| Where you bought | Where the subscription is managed |
| --- | --- |
| **Our website** | On [letterly.app](https://letterly.app), or write to us |
| **App Store** | Apple ID → Subscriptions, on your iPhone or Mac |
| **Google Play** | Google Play → Payments and subscriptions, on your Android device |

<Callout type="note">

Not sure where you bought it? Open **Settings → Current plan** — it shows where your subscription is managed.

<MoreLink href="/check-subscription-status/">Learn more about checking your plan →</MoreLink>

</Callout>

Whichever way you buy, Pro is tied to your Letterly account and works on all your devices.
