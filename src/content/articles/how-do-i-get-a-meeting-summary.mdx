A summary is a rewrite option, so you make one after the recording is transcribed.

<Steps>

1. Open the note with your recording.
2. Tap or click **Rewrite**.
3. Choose a summary option, such as **Short Summary** or **Meeting Takeaways**.

</Steps>

Letterly turns the full transcript into a short recap of the key points. The original transcript is kept — swipe between versions to switch back to it at any time.

<Callout type="tip">

Need the recap in a particular shape, for example decisions and action items only? Start from **Meeting Takeaways**, or create a [custom rewrite option](/custom-rewrite-options/) with your own instructions and reuse it after every meeting.

</Callout>
