<PlatformTabs>
<Platform name="mac">

The **floating bar** is a Letterly control that stays on top of every window. Click it to start dictating without opening the app.

<Callout type="note">

On the **Free** plan: **20 dictations**, each up to **3 minutes**.

<MoreLink href="/what-plans-are-there/">Learn more about plans →</MoreLink>

</Callout>

<Callout type="tip">

The floating bar is optional. Turn it off in **Settings → Dictation → Floating bar** and start dictation with your [shortcut](/shortcuts/) instead.

</Callout>

## Controls while you dictate

<Figure src="/images/help-center/mac-floating-bar-controls.png" alt="Controls on the floating bar" />

| Control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **X** | Discards the current dictation |
| **Timer** | Shows how much time you have left |
| **Stop** (red button) | Ends the recording and processes your text |
| **Pinned rewrite options** | Applies a rewrite option to your dictation. Each option has a keyboard shortcut |
| **Rewrite options menu** | Opens the full list of rewrite options |

## Hide or show the floating bar

<Steps>

1. Go to **Settings → Dictation → Floating bar**.
2. Turn the **Always show** toggle on to keep the bar visible, or off to hide it.

</Steps>

## Hide or show the floating bar

<Steps>

1. Go to **Settings → Dictation → Floating bar**.
2. Turn the **Always show** toggle on to keep the bar visible, or off to hide it.

</Steps>

Your Dictation shortcut works either way.

<Figure src="/images/help-center/windows-floating-bar-settings.png" alt="Floating bar settings on Windows" />

## Change the position of the floating bar

<Steps>

1. Go to **Settings → Dictation → Floating bar → Position**.
2. Choose **Top** or **Bottom**.

</Steps>

<Callout type="tip">

To place the bar anywhere on the screen, choose **Custom** from the floating bar itself, then drag the bar where you want it.

</Callout>

## The right-click menu

Right-click the bar to reach: **Paste last transcript** · **History** · **Microphone** · **Bar position** · **Always show the floating bar** · **Open Letterly** · **Settings** · **Quit Letterly**.

<Callout type="note">

The **Microphone** setting is only in this menu — there is no microphone picker in the Dictation settings.

</Callout>
