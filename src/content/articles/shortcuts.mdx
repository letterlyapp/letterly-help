<Platform name="mac">

The **Dictation shortcut** is the key that starts and stops dictation in any app. The default shortcut is **F5**.

## Two ways to use the shortcut

| Mode | How it works |
| --- | --- |
| **Hold to talk** | Hold the shortcut while you speak, release it to stop |
| **Press to talk** | Press once to start, press again to stop |

## Change the shortcut

You can open the Dictation shortcut screen in two places. Both open the same screen:

- **Settings → Shortcuts**, then click the **Dictation** row
- **Settings → Dictation → Set shortcut**

On that screen, click **Change**, then press the key or key combination you want to use.

<Platform name="windows">

The **Dictation shortcut** is the key combination that starts and stops dictation in any app, even when Letterly is closed. The default shortcut is **Ctrl + Win**.

## Two ways to use the shortcut

| Mode | How it works |
| --- | --- |
| **Hold to talk** | Hold the shortcut while you speak, release it to stop |
| **Press to talk** | Press once to start, press again to stop |

## Change the shortcut

You can open the Dictation shortcut screen in two places. Both open the same screen:

- **Settings → Shortcuts**, then click the **Dictation** row
- **Settings → Floating bar**, then click the **Dictation shortcut** row

On that screen, click **Change**, then press the key combination you want to use.

</Platform>
</PlatformTabs>

<Callout type="note" title="How do I change the dictation shortcut?">

Open **Settings → Dictation**, click the current shortcut, and press the new combination.
