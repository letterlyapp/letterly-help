Recording limits depend on your plan — both how long a single recording can be, and how many notes you can keep.

## Free and Pro

| Plan | Limit |
| --- | --- |
| **Free** | Up to **10 notes** in total, each recording up to **3 minutes** |
| **Pro** | Unlimited notes, each recording up to **90 minutes** |

<Callout type="note">

When a recording reaches its limit — **90 minutes** on Pro, **3 minutes** on Free — Letterly stops and saves it automatically. Nothing is lost.

</Callout>

<Callout type="note">

On Pro you can keep going: open the note and use **Record more** to add to it, as many times as you need. On the Free plan Record more is shown with a **Pro** label.

<MoreLink href="/record-more/">Learn more about Record more →</MoreLink>

</Callout>

You can also make several recordings back to back and combine them — see [Merge notes](/merge-notes/).

## Dictation

On **Pro**, a single dictation can be up to **15 minutes**. On the **Free** plan it is **1 minute**.

Need longer? Record a note in the app instead.

See everything each plan includes in [What plans are there?](/what-plans-are-there/)
