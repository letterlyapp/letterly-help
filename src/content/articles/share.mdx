You can send a note to another app without leaving Letterly — as text or as the original recording.

<Callout type="tip">

To send one note to a person or another app, see **Share**. To get your notes out of Letterly in bulk or automatically, see [Export notes](/export-notes/).

</Callout>

## Copy the text

Tap the **copy** button in the note. The text is placed on your clipboard, ready to paste anywhere.

## Share the transcript

<Steps>

1. Open the note and tap the **⋯** menu.
2. Choose **Share with**.
3. Pick an app — Messages, Mail, Notion, or anything else in your share sheet.

</Steps>

<Video src="/images/help-center/share-note.mp4" caption="Sharing a note" />

<Callout type="tip">

Letterly shares the version you are viewing. Switch to the version you need before you share it.

</Callout>

## Share the recording

<Steps>

1. Open the note and tap the **⋯** menu.
2. Choose **Export audio**.
3. Save the file, or send it straight to another app.

</Steps>

The file is named after the note title, so it stays recognizable outside Letterly.
