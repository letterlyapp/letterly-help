<Platform name="iphone">

When you install Letterly and open it, you go through a short setup.

## Step 1. Start

The welcome screen explains what Letterly does. Tap **Let's start!**

<Callout type="note">

Already have an account? Tap **I have an account** at the bottom and sign in with the email you used before.

</Callout>

## Step 2. Tell Letterly what you need it for

Pick what you plan to write — messages and emails, notes, thoughts and ideas, journaling, social media posts. You can choose several.

This helps us shape Letterly around the way you actually use it.

<Figure src="/images/help-center/onboarding-iphone-what-to-do.png" alt="Choose what you plan to write with Letterly" caption="Choose what you plan to write with Letterly" />

On this screen iOS also asks whether Letterly may track your activity across other apps. It is optional and changes nothing in how the app works — we use it only to see which ad channel you came from.

<Figure src="/images/help-center/onboarding-iphone-tracking.png" alt="The iOS tracking prompt" caption="The iOS tracking prompt" />

## Step 3. Create an account

Choose **Continue with Google**, **Continue with Email**, or **Continue with Apple**. An account keeps your notes safe and available on every device you use Letterly on.

<Figure src="/images/help-center/onboarding-iphone-create-account.png" alt="Create an account or log in" caption="Create an account or log in" />

<Callout type="note">

If you already have a subscription, sign in with the email you bought it with. Otherwise you start on the Free plan.

<MoreLink href="/what-plans-are-there/">Learn more about plans →</MoreLink>

</Callout>

You can also continue without an account and sign in later — but your notes will not sync to your other devices until you do.

## Step 4. Allow microphone access

Letterly needs the microphone to transcribe your speech. Tap **Continue**, then allow it in the iOS prompt. Without it, recording does not work.

<Callout type="note">

Declined by accident? You can turn it on later.

<MoreLink href="/permissions/">Learn more about Permissions →</MoreLink>

</Callout>

## Step 5. Set up Dictation

The **Letterly Dictation** screen offers writing with your voice in any app, without opening Letterly. Tap **Set up** to do it now, or **Later** to skip.

<Callout type="tip">

You can always come back to it from **Settings → Dictation**.

<MoreLink href="/set-up-dictation/">Learn more about Set up Dictation →</MoreLink>

</Callout>

## You are in

You land on the notes screen. Tap the microphone button to make your first recording. If you skipped dictation, the **Letterly Dictation** banner stays at the top until you set it up.

</Platform>

When you install Letterly and open it, you go through a short setup.

## Step 1. Start

The welcome screen explains what Letterly does. Tap **Let's start!**

<Callout type="note">

Already have an account? Tap **I have an account** at the bottom and sign in with the email you used before.

</Callout>

## Step 2. Tell Letterly what you need it for

Pick what you plan to write — messages and emails, notes, thoughts and ideas, journaling, social media posts. You can choose several.

This helps us shape Letterly around the way you actually use it.

## Step 3. Create an account

Tap **Continue with Google** or **Continue with Email**.

<Callout type="note">

If you already have a subscription, sign in with the email you bought it with. Otherwise you start on the Free plan.

<MoreLink href="/what-plans-are-there/">Learn more about plans →</MoreLink>

</Callout>

You can also continue without an account and sign in later — but your notes will not sync to your other devices until you do.

## Step 4. Allow permissions

Android asks for up to three permissions, one after another.

| Permission | Why Letterly asks |
| --- | --- |
| **Microphone** | Required. Without it Letterly cannot record. Choose **While using the app** |
| **Nearby devices** | Optional. Lets Letterly work with Bluetooth headphones |
| **Notifications** | Optional, but useful — this is how Letterly tells you about your account and new features |

<Callout type="note">

Declined something by accident? You can change it later.

<MoreLink href="/permissions/">Learn more about Permissions →</MoreLink>

</Callout>

## Step 5. Set up Dictation

The last screen offers **Letterly Dictation** — writing with your voice in any app, without opening Letterly. Tap **Set up** to do it now, or **Later** to skip.

If you skip, Letterly asks you to confirm: dictation will not work until the setup is finished, and you can come back to it at any time.

<Callout type="tip">

Come back to it from **Settings → Dictation**.

<MoreLink href="/set-up-dictation/">Learn more about Set up Dictation →</MoreLink>

</Callout>

## You are in

You land on the notes screen. Tap the microphone button to make your first recording. If you skipped dictation, the **Letterly Dictation** banner stays at the top until you set it up.

<Platform name="windows">

When you open Letterly on Windows for the first time, you go through a short setup. Signing in happens first — the app works only with an account.

## Step 1. Start the setup

The welcome screen explains what Letterly does. Click **Let's start!** Letterly opens the sign-in page in your web browser.

## Step 2. Sign in

What you see depends on whether you are already signed in to Letterly on the web.

**Already signed in:** the **Open the Letterly desktop app** screen shows your account. Check that it is the right one and click **Continue**. If it is not, click **Use a different account**.

**Not signed in yet:** choose **Continue with Google**, **Continue with Apple**, or **Continue with Email**.

<Callout type="note">

If you already have a subscription, sign in with the email you bought it with. Otherwise you start on the Free plan.

<MoreLink href="/what-plans-are-there/">Learn more about plans →</MoreLink>

</Callout>

## Step 3. Return to the app

The browser shows **Authentication complete** and asks to open Letterly. Click **Open Letterly** in the dialog, then close the browser tab.

<Callout type="tip">

Tick **Always allow web.letterly.app to open links of this type** and Windows will skip this dialog next time.

</Callout>

## Step 4. Meet Dictation

The app opens on the **Meet Dictation** screen — dictation lets you speak directly in any app and see your words appear right there.

Click **Try now** to set it up, or **Later** to skip.

<Callout type="tip">

You can always come back through **Settings → Dictation**.

<MoreLink href="/set-up-dictation/">Learn more about Set up Dictation →</MoreLink>

</Callout>

## You are in

You land on the notes screen. Click the record button to make your first note.

</Platform>
