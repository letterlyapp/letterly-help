You can move between the monthly, annual and lifetime plans at any time — for example, from monthly to annual, or from annual to lifetime.

<Callout type="warn">

Cancel your current subscription before purchasing a new one — otherwise both remain active and are billed separately.

</Callout>

## Switch to another plan

<Steps>

1. **Cancel your current plan** where you bought it — see [Manage or cancel my subscription](/manage-or-cancel-my-subscription/).
2. **Buy the plan you want.**

</Steps>

<Callout type="tip">

You can also write to [hi@letterly.app](mailto:hi@letterly.app) and we will switch the plan for you.

</Callout>

## Change your billing cycle

Switching between monthly and annual billing works the same way. Write to [hi@letterly.app](mailto:hi@letterly.app) and we will change it for you.

<Callout type="note" title="Bought on our website?">

Manage everything in the subscription portal. Sign in with the **same email you used for the purchase** — otherwise the portal will not find your subscription.

</Callout>
