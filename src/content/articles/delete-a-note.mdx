There are two ways to delete a note.

### Option 1: Open the note

<Steps>

1. Open the note and tap the **⋯** menu.
2. Choose **Delete this note**.

</Steps>

### Option 2: Use the notes list

<Steps>

1. Press and hold the note in the list.
2. Choose **Delete**.

</Steps>

<Callout type="warn">

Deleting is permanent. The note, all of its versions, and its audio are removed and cannot be restored.

</Callout>

## Archive instead

To move a note out of your way without losing it, open the note, tap or click **⋯**, and choose **Archive**. Archived notes are kept and stay searchable.

## Delete a single version

To remove one rewrite and keep the rest of the note, use **Delete this rewrite** in the rewrite menu.
