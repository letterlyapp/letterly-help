Sorting controls the order of notes on the notes screen.

## Change the order

<Steps>

1. On the notes screen, tap the **sort** button in the top left.
2. Choose what to sort by.
3. Choose the direction — newest first or oldest first.

</Steps>

| Option | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Last updated** | Notes you edited or rewrote most recently come first |
| **Creation date** | Notes appear in the order you recorded them |

Your choice is remembered until you change it.
