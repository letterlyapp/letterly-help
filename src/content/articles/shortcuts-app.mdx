Letterly comes with Apple Shortcuts that start a recording without opening the app. Add a shortcut once, then run it from the **Action Button**, **Control Center**, the **Lock Screen**, or the **Home Screen**.

<Callout type="warn">

Requires iOS 26 or newer.

</Callout>

## Two shortcuts

| Shortcut | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Quick Voice Note** | Records and saves the note in Letterly |
| **Dictate to Clipboard** | Records in the background so you can dictate into another app |

## Add a shortcut

<Steps>

1. In Letterly, open **Settings → Dictation → Action button**.
2. Tap **Add Shortcut**.
3. Confirm adding it in the Shortcuts app.

</Steps>

## Put it on your Action Button

<Steps>

1. Open **Settings → Action Button**.
2. Swipe to **Shortcut**.
3. Select the Letterly shortcut you added.

</Steps>

<Callout type="tip">

The Action Button is not the only place. You can also add the shortcut to **Control Center**, the **Lock Screen**, or your **Home Screen** — anywhere iOS lets you place a shortcut.

</Callout>

## Record a note without opening Letterly

Run the shortcut, say what is on your mind, and the note appears in Letterly on its own. Your phone can stay locked the whole time.

## Dictate into another app

<Steps>

1. Place your cursor in the app you are writing in — a messenger, mail, a form.
2. Run the shortcut. The recording runs in the background, so the app stays open.
3. Stop the recording from the **Letterly Keyboard**. The text lands in the field you are in.

</Steps>

<Callout type="tip">

Your dictation is also copied to the clipboard, so you can paste it anywhere if it was not inserted.

</Callout>
