<Platform name="iphone">

When you install Letterly, your device asks for a few permissions. Only one of them is required.

## Microphone

Letterly needs the microphone to record your voice. Without it, recording and dictation do not work. The prompt appears during [Onboarding](/onboarding/), the first time you record.

Declined it by accident? Turn it on here:

<Steps>

1. Open **Settings** on your iPhone.
2. Scroll to **Letterly** and tap it.
3. Turn on **Microphone**.

</Steps>

## Notifications

Optional, but useful — this is how Letterly tells you about your account, your subscription, and new features. You can turn notifications off at any time in your device settings.

## Tracking

iOS asks whether apps may track you across other apps and websites. Letterly works exactly the same either way, so answer however you prefer.

<Callout type="safe">

Whatever you choose here, Letterly does not sell your data or use your recordings to train AI models.

</Callout>

<Platform name="android">

When you install Letterly, your device asks for a few permissions. Only one of them is required.

## Microphone

Letterly needs the microphone to record your voice. Without it, recording and dictation do not work. The prompt appears during [Onboarding](/onboarding/), the first time you record.

Declined it by accident? Turn it on here:

<Steps>

1. Open **Settings → Apps → Letterly**.
2. Tap **Permissions → Microphone**.
3. Choose **Allow**.

</Steps>

## Nearby devices

Optional. Android asks for it so Letterly can work with Bluetooth headphones. Recording from the phone's own microphone works either way.
