<Platform name="iphone">

Letterly has a **Free** plan and a **Pro** plan.

|   | Free | Pro |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Notes** | 10 | Unlimited |
| **Recording length in the app** | 3 minutes | 90 minutes |
| **Record more** | ❌ | ✅ |
| **Upload audio** | ❌ | ✅ |
| **Dictations** | 20 | Unlimited |
| **Dictation length** | 1 minute | 15 minutes |
| **Apple Watch** | ❌ | ✅ |
| **Rewrite options** | Magic only, 10 rewrites | All options, unlimited |
| **Devices** | All your devices | All your devices |

**The same on both plans:** translation, multi-speaker, Dictionary, text replacements, export, share, downloading audio, integrations, Letterly MCP and the Shortcuts app.

Pro comes as a **monthly**, **annual** or **lifetime** plan. One subscription covers all your devices.
