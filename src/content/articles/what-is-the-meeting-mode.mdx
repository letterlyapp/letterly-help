Use **Meeting** mode to record a conversation or a talk — a meeting, a lecture, an interview. Letterly records through the microphone and turns the recording into a full transcript you can search, rewrite, and share.

## How it works

<Steps>

1. Open Letterly and tap the **record** button.
2. Turn on **Multi-speaker** so the transcript is split by who said what.
3. Place your device where it can hear everyone, and start the conversation.
4. Tap or click **Stop**. Letterly transcribes the recording and creates a note.
5. Tap or click **Rewrite** and choose a summary option to get a short recap of the key points.

</Steps>

<Callout type="note">

One recording can be up to **90 minutes** long on **Pro**, and up to **3 minutes** on the **Free** plan. If the meeting runs longer, use [Record more](/record-more/) to continue in the same note.

</Callout>

<Callout type="note" title="Ask before you record.">

Tell the other participants that you are recording and wait for their consent.

</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="Forgot to turn on Multi-speaker before the meeting?">

You can still do it afterwards. Open the note, tap or click **⋯**, and choose **Add speaker roles** — Letterly goes through the recording again and splits it by speaker.

</Callout>

## What you get

| Result | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Transcript** | Everything that was said, as text |
| **Speakers** | The transcript split by speaker |
| **Summary** | A short recap of the key points, from any summary rewrite option |
| **Audio** | The original recording, saved in the note |

## What people use it for

- Team meetings and 1:1s
- Lectures, classes, and talks
- Interviews and research calls
- Long conversations you want to keep, not retype

<Callout type="warn">

Letterly records through the microphone, so it does not capture call audio directly. See [Can I record a Zoom or Google Meet call?](/can-i-record-a-zoom-or-google-meet-call/) and [Can I record a phone call?](/can-i-record-a-phone-call/)

</Callout>
