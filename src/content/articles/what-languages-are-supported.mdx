## Languages you can speak in

Letterly recognises speech in most major languages, and you do not need to choose one before you record — it detects the language on its own.

If it gets the language wrong, set it manually. This is the **speaker's language**.

**For one note:** open the note, tap or click **⋯**, choose **Speaker's language**, and pick the language. Letterly transcribes the recording again.

**For future notes:** open **Settings → Speaker's language**. This does not change notes you already have.

<Callout type="note">

Set the speaker's language when you have an accent, or when you switch between languages inside one recording — accuracy improves noticeably.

</Callout>

## Translating a note

The speaker's language is about what Letterly hears. Translation is a separate feature that converts a finished note into another language, and the original stays untouched.

<Callout type="note">

Letterly translates a note into 57 languages.

</Callout>

## Interface language

The Letterly interface is available in English, Spanish, Italian, German, French, Portuguese, Japanese, Dutch, Russian and Thai.

Letterly follows your device: change the system language on your phone or computer, and the app switches to it automatically. You can also set it yourself in **Settings → Interface language**.

<Callout type="note">

The interface language does not affect transcription. You can use Letterly in English and dictate in Japanese.

</Callout>
