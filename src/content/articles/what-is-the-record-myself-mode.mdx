<Platform name="iphone">

In **Record myself** mode you record inside Letterly. Every recording becomes a note with a clean transcript that you can rewrite, organize, and reuse — a voice notepad that turns talking into finished text.

## How it works

<Steps>

1. Open Letterly and tap the **record** button on the notes screen.
2. Say what is on your mind. Pauses and filler words are fine — Letterly removes them.
3. Tap **Stop**. Letterly transcribes the recording and creates a note with a title.
4. Tap **Rewrite** to turn the transcript into the format you need.

</Steps>

<Callout type="tip">

You can also start a recording without opening the app: ask [Siri](/siri/), press the [Action button](/action-button/), tap the [Widget](/widget/) on your Home or Lock Screen, or use your [Apple Watch](/apple-watch/).

</Callout>

## What people use it for

- Quick notes and to-dos
- Journaling and thinking out loud
- Drafting posts, emails, and longer writing
- Capturing an idea before it disappears

<Callout type="tip">

Want the text to appear directly in another app instead of a note? Use [Dictation](/what-is-the-dictation-mode/).

</Callout>
