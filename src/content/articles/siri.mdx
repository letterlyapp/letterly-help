Siri can open Letterly and start a recording for you, without touching the screen.

<Callout type="tip">

The wording and menu names can look different depending on your iOS version.

</Callout>

## 1. Confirm the basics

- **Internet connection** — Siri needs a working connection for most requests.
- **Letterly app** — make sure it is installed and you are logged in.

## 2. Make sure Siri is turned on

Go to **Settings → Siri** (or **Siri & Search**) **→ Talk to Siri** and choose **“Siri”** or **“Hey Siri”**, not **Off**.

<Callout type="warn">

If voice activation is off, Siri only works through the side button, not by voice.

</Callout>

## 3. Set Siri's language

Open **Settings → Siri** (or **Siri & Search**) **→ Language** and select the language you will actually speak.

## Start using Siri with Letterly

| Say this | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| *“Hey Siri, open Letterly”* | Opens the app on the main screen with all your notes |
| *“Hey Siri, start recording in Letterly”*<br />*“Hey Siri, record a voice note in Letterly”* | Letterly jumps straight into a new audio recording |
| *“Hey Siri, new note in Letterly”*<br />*“Hey Siri, text note in Letterly”* | Letterly opens a fresh note ready for typing |
