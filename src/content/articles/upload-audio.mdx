<PlatformTabs>
<Platform name="iphone">

Already have a recording? Add it to Letterly and it is transcribed like a note you recorded in the app — with a title, a transcript, and rewrite options.

<Callout type="note">

**Upload audio** is available on **Pro**.

<MoreLink href="/what-plans-are-there/">Learn more about plans →</MoreLink>

</Callout>

## Upload a file

<Steps>

1. On the notes screen, tap **+**.
2. Choose **Upload audio**.
3. Pick the file.

</Steps>

<Figure src="/images/help-center/upload-audio-iphone.png" alt="+ → Upload audio" caption="+ → Upload audio" />

<Callout type="note">

Letterly transcribes the file and creates a note. You can leave the app while it works.

</Callout>

<Callout type="tip">

The **+** menu also has **Write note**, for a note you type by hand. See [Write a text note](/write-a-text-note/).

</Callout>

## Send audio from another app

In the app where the recording is — Voice Memos, WhatsApp, your recorder — use **Share** and choose **Letterly**.

</Platform>
