Export takes your content out of Letterly — one recording, or every note you have.

<Callout type="tip">

To send one note to a person or another app, see [Share](/share/). To get your notes out of Letterly in bulk or automatically, see **Export notes**.

</Callout>

## Export a recording

<Steps>

1. Open the note and tap the **⋯** menu.
2. Choose **Export audio**.
3. Save the file.

</Steps>

The file is named after the note title. You can export a recording from any device where you are signed in, not only the one you recorded on — see [Download audio](/download-audio/).

## Export the text

Letterly does not have a bulk text export in the app. For one note, copy the text or use **Share with** — see [Share](/share/).

## Send notes out automatically

Connect Letterly to the tools you already use, and new notes arrive there without any manual steps:

| Method | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| [Zapier](/integrations/) | Sends notes to 8,000+ apps — Google Docs, Notion, Slack, and more |
| [Webhooks](/integrations/) | Sends notes to your own endpoint |
| [Letterly MCP](/letterly-mcp/) | Lets AI tools such as Claude and ChatGPT read your notes directly |

## Export everything

For a full export of all your notes, email [hi@letterly.app](mailto:hi@letterly.app) and we will prepare it for you.

<Callout type="tip">

Planning to delete your account? Export your notes first — deletion removes them permanently. See [How do I delete my account and data?](/how-do-i-delete-my-account-and-data/)

</Callout>
