**Letterly MCP** connects your notes to AI tools, so you can **search, rewrite, translate and create notes right inside your AI conversation** — without leaving it.

## What you can do with it

- Ask your AI tool to find a note by what you said in it.
- Rewrite or translate an existing note without opening Letterly.
- Create a new note straight from the conversation.

## Supported tools

Letterly MCP works with **Claude**, **Claude Code**, **ChatGPT**, **Cursor**, **Perplexity**, **Gemini**, **Codex**, **VS Code**, **Windsurf** and any other MCP-compatible client.

Setup guides for each tool, the endpoint and the full list of available actions are on [letterly.app/mcp](https://letterly.app/mcp/).

<Callout type="note">

MCP is a different thing from **Integrations**. MCP lets an AI tool work with your notes; Integrations send a finished note somewhere else.
