## Open Dictation

On the Home screen, tap **Set up** on the **Letterly Dictation** banner. If you tapped **Later** before, open **Settings → Dictation** and tap **Set up** there.

<Figure src="/images/blog/how-to-use-letterly-keyboard-1.webp" alt="The Letterly Dictation banner on the Home screen" caption="The Letterly Dictation banner on the Home screen" />

<Figure src="/images/blog/how-to-use-letterly-keyboard-2.webp" alt="Or start the setup from Settings → Dictation" caption="Or start the setup from Settings → Dictation" />

## Allow permissions

### Add the Letterly Keyboard

<Steps>

1. In Letterly, tap **Open Settings**.
2. In your iPhone settings, tap **Keyboards**.
3. Turn on **Letterly**.

</Steps>

<Callout type="tip">

New to Letterly? If you are prompted, allow microphone access before you start dictating.

</Callout>

<Figure src="/images/blog/how-to-use-letterly-keyboard-3.webp" alt="Add the Letterly Keyboard in iPhone settings" caption="Add the Letterly Keyboard in iPhone settings" />

### Enable Full Access

After you turn on the **Letterly Keyboard**, the **Allow Full Access** option appears below it on the same screen. Turn it on.

Full Access is **a technical requirement** that allows the keyboard to process your dictation and return the finished text. **Without this permission, Dictation cannot work.** Apple shows a standard warning for every third-party keyboard that uses Full Access — it describes what the permission technically allows, not how Letterly handles your data.

<Callout type="safe">

Letterly does not misuse, sell, or share your data. Your Dictation History is stored locally on your device, and no one on the Letterly team has direct access to your personal notes or dictated content.

</Callout>

<Figure src="/images/blog/how-to-use-letterly-keyboard-4.webp" alt="Enable Full Access for the Letterly Keyboard" caption="Enable Full Access for the Letterly Keyboard" />

### Return to Letterly

After you turn on **Allow Full Access**, tap **Letterly** in the top-left corner to return to the app and complete the setup. You can now dictate in Messages, Mail, Notes, WhatsApp, ChatGPT, and other apps.

<Figure src="/images/blog/how-to-use-letterly-keyboard-4-1.webp" alt="Tap Letterly in the top-left corner to return to the app" caption="Tap Letterly in the top-left corner to return to the app" />

## Use Dictation

### Open the Letterly Keyboard

Tap any text field, then use the **globe icon** to switch to the Letterly Keyboard.

### Start dictating

Tap the **microphone** button. Recording begins immediately — there is no extra confirmation step.

<Callout type="warn">

When you start Dictation from another app, iOS briefly opens Letterly and then returns you to the app you were typing in. iOS requires this switch — see [Dictation switches to Letterly](/dictation-switches-to-letterly/).

</Callout>

<Figure src="/images/blog/how-to-use-letterly-keyboard-5.webp" alt="Recording controls on the Letterly Keyboard" caption="Recording controls on the Letterly Keyboard" />

| Control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **X** | Cancels and discards the current recording |
| **Timer** | Shows how much time you have left |
| **Stop** (red button) | Finishes the recording and inserts the text |
| **Restart** | Discards the recording and starts again |

<Callout type="note">

A single dictation can be up to **15 minutes**. Need longer? Record a note in the app instead — up to 90 minutes.

</Callout>

### Rewrite the text

The **Rewrite** button appears when Letterly has just inserted your dictated text, or when you select existing text. Tap it to choose an option such as **Magic**, **List**, **Structured** or **Journal Entry**.

<Figure src="/images/blog/how-to-use-letterly-keyboard-6.webp" alt="The rewrite options menu" caption="The rewrite options menu" />

- **Rewrite by voice** — say how you want to change the text, or correct mistakes out loud.
- **Favorites** — the options you pinned, at the top of the menu.
- **Text Editing** — the ready-made options from Letterly.

The button disappears when you change the text — select the text again to rewrite it.

### Use the keyboard menu

Tap the menu button on the left for extra actions.

<Figure src="/images/blog/how-to-use-letterly-keyboard-7.webp" alt="Actions in the keyboard menu" caption="Actions in the keyboard menu" />

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Undo** | Removes the text Letterly just inserted |
| **Redo** | Restores the text removed with Undo |
| **Add to Dictionary** | Adds a word to your [Dictionary](/dictionary/) so Letterly spells it correctly next time |
| **History** | Opens your Dictation History in Letterly |

### Turn Dictation off

On the Letterly Home screen, tap the **power icon** next to **Dictation**. You can also do it from the Lock Screen — tap the power icon in the Letterly widget without unlocking your iPhone.

<Figure src="/images/blog/how-to-use-letterly-keyboard-9.webp" alt="Turn Dictation off from the Home screen" caption="Turn Dictation off from the Home screen" />

## Dictation history

Letterly saves every completed dictation in **History**, grouped by date. Open it from the keyboard menu, or in the app under **Settings → History**.

<Figure src="/images/blog/how-to-use-letterly-keyboard-10.webp" alt="Two ways to open Dictation History" caption="Two ways to open Dictation History" />

Press and hold a dictation, or swipe left on it, to see the actions.

<Figure src="/images/blog/how-to-use-letterly-keyboard-11.webp" alt="Actions in Dictation History" caption="Actions in Dictation History" />

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Copy** | Copies the dictation text to your clipboard |
| **Export audio** | Saves or shares the original recording as an audio file |
| **Delete** | Permanently removes the dictation from History |

<Callout type="warn">

Letterly asks you to confirm before deleting. Once deleted, a dictation cannot be restored.

</Callout>

<Callout type="note">

Dictation History stays on the device where it was created and does not sync to your other devices.

<MoreLink href="/history/">Learn more about Dictation History →</MoreLink>

</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="Make Dictation your own">

Letterly can clean up your text automatically after every dictation, and keep your favourite rewrite options at the top of the keyboard menu. Set both in the app — see [Dictation output](/dictation-output/) and [Favorite rewrite options](/favorite-rewrite-options/).

</Callout>

## Allow permissions

You will grant two permissions: **Display over other apps**, so the Bubble can appear, and **Accessibility**, so Letterly can insert your text.

### Allow the Bubble to appear over other apps

Tap **Continue** next to **Show the Dictation Bubble**, then tap **Open Settings**.

<Figure src="/images/blog/how-to-use-dictation-bubble-on-android-1.webp" alt="Tap Continue, then Open Settings" caption="Tap Continue, then Open Settings" />

<Steps>

1. Find and tap **Letterly**.
2. Turn on **Allow display over other apps**.

</Steps>

<Figure src="/images/blog/how-to-use-dictation-bubble-on-android-2.webp" alt="Turn on Allow display over other apps for Letterly" caption="Turn on Allow display over other apps for Letterly" />

<Callout type="note" title="Why Letterly needs this permission">

It allows the Bubble to appear above WhatsApp, Gmail, Messages, ChatGPT and other apps. Without it, the Bubble cannot appear outside Letterly.

</Callout>

### Allow Letterly to insert text

Tap **Open Settings** next to **Insert text in any app**. Letterly explains how it uses Accessibility Services — review it and tap **Continue**.

<Callout type="safe">

Letterly uses Android's AccessibilityService API only to detect the active text field and insert your dictated text. It does **not** read passwords, payment or banking fields, record calls, change system settings, or control your device without your action.

</Callout>

<Figure src="/images/blog/how-to-use-dictation-bubble-on-android-3.webp" alt="Review how Letterly uses Accessibility, then tap Continue" caption="Review how Letterly uses Accessibility, then tap Continue" />

<Steps>

1. Tap **Downloaded apps**.
2. Tap **Letterly**.
3. Turn on **Use Letterly**.

</Steps>

<Figure src="/images/blog/how-to-use-dictation-bubble-on-android-4.webp" alt="Turn on Use Letterly under Accessibility" caption="Turn on Use Letterly under Accessibility" />

### Confirm the Android warning

Some devices show an extra system warning, because Accessibility is a sensitive permission. Android shows it for any app that uses Accessibility Services.

<Callout type="safe">

This is a **standard system notice**. It does not describe Letterly's privacy practices — we do not sell your data, share it with third parties for their own use, or use it to train AI models.

</Callout>

<Steps>

1. Wait for the countdown to finish, if one appears.
2. Select **I'm aware of the possible risks**.
3. Tap **OK**, then return to Letterly.

</Steps>

<Figure src="/images/blog/how-to-use-dictation-bubble-on-android-5.webp" alt="Confirm the Android Accessibility warning" caption="Confirm the Android Accessibility warning" />

## Allow permissions

Letterly guides you through two permissions: **Microphone**, so it can hear what you dictate, and **Accessibility**, so it can insert the finished text into the active field.

<Callout type="note">

If you have used Letterly before, microphone access may already be enabled — that step is skipped automatically.

1. Click **Allow** in Letterly.
2. When the **No microphone access** message appears, click **Settings**.
3. Enable Letterly under **Microphone**, then return to Letterly.
4. Click **Allow** again and enable Letterly under **Accessibility**.
5. If macOS asks you to, quit and reopen Letterly.

</Steps>

## Customize Dictation

Open **Settings → Dictation → Floating bar**. You can come back and change these settings at any time.

<Figure src="/images/blog/how-to-set-up-letterly-dictation-on-mac-3.webp" alt="Dictation settings on Mac" caption="Dictation settings on Mac" />

### Set shortcut

By default, Dictation starts when you press **F5**. Hold it while speaking and release to stop, or tap once to start and again to stop. See [Shortcuts](/shortcuts/).

<Callout type="note">

Letterly does not support a modifier key such as **Option** or **Command** on its own.

</Callout>

### The floating bar

A small control that stays on top of any window. Click it to start dictating without opening the app. It is optional — with it turned off, your shortcut still works.

<Figure src="/images/blog/how-to-set-up-letterly-dictation-on-mac-4.webp" alt="Controls on the floating bar" caption="Controls on the floating bar" />

| Control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **X** | Discards the current dictation |
| **Timer** | Shows how much time you have left |
| **Stop** (red button) | Ends the recording and processes your text |
| **Pinned rewrite options** | Applies a rewrite option. Each one has a keyboard shortcut |
| **Rewrite options menu** | Opens the full list of rewrite options |

<Callout type="note">

If the audio bars are moving, Letterly is listening. If they stay still while you speak, check that Letterly has microphone permission.

</Callout>

### Dictation output

By default Letterly uses **System**, which keeps the text close to what you said while removing filler words and adding punctuation. Choose any option as your main one — **Structured**, **List**, **Short Summary**, **Journal Entry** — or pick **Raw Transcript** to keep your words untouched.

**Pinned rewrite options** sit right on the floating bar. Pin them in **Settings → Dictation → Floating bar → Pinned rewrite options**. See [Dictation output](/dictation-output/).

<Figure src="/images/blog/how-to-set-up-letterly-dictation-on-mac-5.webp" alt="Choose your pinned rewrite options" caption="Choose your pinned rewrite options" />

## Use Dictation

Click where you want the text to appear — an email, a message, a document — then press your shortcut or click the floating bar.

<Figure src="/images/blog/how-to-set-up-letterly-dictation-on-mac-6.webp" alt="Place your cursor before you dictate" caption="Place your cursor before you dictate" />

<Callout type="note">

A single dictation can be up to **15 minutes**. Need longer? Record a note in the app instead — up to 90 minutes.

</Callout>

<Callout type="warn">

If there is no active cursor, Letterly does not know where to insert the text, so your dictation is copied to the clipboard instead. Place your cursor and press **Command + V**.

</Callout>

<Callout type="note">

### Set shortcut

The key combination that starts dictation. The default is **Ctrl + Win** — hold it for something short, or press once to start and again to stop. See [Shortcuts](/shortcuts/).

<Figure src="/images/blog/how-to-set-up-letterly-dictation-on-windows-8.webp" alt="Change the shortcut that triggers Dictation" caption="Change the shortcut that triggers Dictation" />

### Always show the floating bar

When on, the floating bar stays visible at all times. Turn it off if you would rather it only appear when needed — your shortcut still works either way.

Even when it is always on, the bar stays out of your way — a thin pill resting at the edge of the screen.

<Video src="/video/letterly-windows-floating-bar.mp4" caption="The floating bar at rest" />

### Keep transcription in clipboard

When this is on, every finished dictation is also copied to your clipboard, so the text is ready to paste with **Ctrl + V**. It works as a safety net: if there was no cursor in a text field, nothing is lost — click where you need it and paste.

<Callout type="note">

Only the latest dictation is kept. Each new one replaces the previous.

</Callout>

### Position

Where the bar sits on your screen: **Top** or **Bottom**. There is also **Custom** — pick it from the bar itself, then drag the bar anywhere.

### Dictation output

By default Letterly uses **System**, which keeps the text close to what you said while removing filler words and adding punctuation. Choose any option as your main one, or pick **Raw Transcript** to keep your words untouched.

**Pinned rewrite options** sit right on the floating bar. Pin them in **Settings → Dictation → Floating bar → Pinned rewrite options**. See [Dictation output](/dictation-output/).
