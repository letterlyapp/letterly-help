**Dictionary** teaches Letterly how to spell individual words — names, brands, technical terms, abbreviations. Add a word once, and Letterly uses your spelling in future notes instead of correcting it every time.

<Callout type="tip" title="Dictionary fixes spelling. It does not add text.">

If you want to say a short phrase and get a longer piece of text instead — a signature, an address, a meeting link — use [Text replacements](/text-replacements/).

</Callout>

## Add a word

<Steps>

1. Open **Settings → Dictionary**.
2. Type the word in the **Add a word** field.
3. Tap **+**.

</Steps>

The word appears in your Dictionary right away. Useful things to add:

- a person's name, such as *Andranik*
- a brand, such as *Quickfile*
- a product name, such as *Letterly*
- a technical term or abbreviation you use often

## Add several words at once

Enter or paste the words on separate lines, then tap **+**. On desktop, press **Shift + Enter** to start a new line.

Each line is saved as a separate entry, and words that are already in your Dictionary are not duplicated.

<Callout type="tip">

Words can be in any language or alphabet. Each entry can be up to 60 characters long.

</Callout>

## Add a word from a note

<Steps>

1. Select the word in the note.
2. Choose **Add to Dictionary**.

</Steps>

The word is saved immediately, so the next recording comes out with the right spelling.

<Callout type="tip">

In the Letterly Keyboard, **Add to Dictionary** is in the keyboard menu — see [Keyboard](/keyboard/).

</Callout>

## Find, edit, or delete words

To find a word, start typing in the field at the bottom of the Dictionary screen — the list filters as you type. Clear the field to see the full list again. If nothing matches, you can add the typed word from there.

To change a word, tap it, or swipe left and choose **Edit**. To remove it, swipe left and choose **Delete**.

<Callout type="warn">

Deleting is permanent — Letterly asks you to confirm first. Existing notes are not changed; the Dictionary applies to new recordings.

</Callout>
