The **Action button** on the side of your iPhone can start Letterly with one press — no unlocking, no searching for the app. You set it up once, using a Letterly shortcut.

## Choose what the button does

| Shortcut | What one press does |
| --- | --- |
| **Quick Voice Note** | Records and saves the note in Letterly |
| **Dictate to Clipboard** | Records in the background so you can dictate into another app |

## Set it up

<Steps>

1. In Letterly, open **Settings → Dictation → Action button**.
2. Tap **Add Shortcut** and confirm adding it in the Shortcuts app.
3. Open **Settings → Action Button** on your iPhone.
4. Swipe to **Shortcut**.
5. Select the Letterly shortcut you added.

</Steps>

## Use it

Press and hold the Action button. Letterly starts recording straight away — the phone does not need to be unlocked.

<Callout type="tip">

Prefer another way to start? The same shortcuts work from **Control Center**, the **Lock Screen**, and your **Home Screen**. See [Shortcuts app](/shortcuts-app/).

</Callout>

<Callout type="warn">

Shortcuts require iOS 26 or newer. On earlier versions, assign the Action button to open Letterly instead.

</Callout>
