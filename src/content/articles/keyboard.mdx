The **Letterly Keyboard** lets you dictate and rewrite text in any app. Add it to your device first — see [Set up Dictation](/set-up-dictation/).

<Callout type="note">

On the **Free** plan: **20 dictations**, each up to **1 minute**, and **10 rewrites** in total. The keyboard shows how many you have left.

<MoreLink href="/what-plans-are-there/">Learn more about plans →</MoreLink>

</Callout>

## Dictate with the keyboard

<Steps>

1. Tap any text field, then use the **globe icon** to switch to the Letterly Keyboard.
2. Tap the **microphone** button. Recording starts immediately.
3. Say what you want to write.
4. Tap the **Stop** button. Letterly inserts the finished text at the cursor.

</Steps>

<Callout type="warn">

When you start dictation from another app, iOS opens Letterly briefly and returns you to the app you were typing in. iOS requires this switch for Dictation to work — see [Dictation switches to Letterly](/dictation-switches-to-letterly/).

</Callout>

While you record, you see these controls:

<Figure src="/images/blog/how-to-use-letterly-keyboard-5.webp" alt="Recording controls on the Letterly Keyboard" caption="Recording controls on the Letterly Keyboard" />

| Control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **X** | Discards the current recording |
| **Timer** | Shows how much time you have left |
| **Stop** (red button) | Ends the recording and inserts the text into the active field |
| **Restart** | Discards the recording and starts again |

<Callout type="note">

A single dictation can be up to **15 minutes**. Need longer? Record a note in the app instead — up to 90 minutes.

</Callout>

## Rewrite text

Right after Letterly inserts your text — or when you select existing text — a **Rewrite** button appears in the keyboard. Tap it to reshape the text into **Magic**, **List**, **Structured**, **Journal Entry** and more. Full details in [Dictation output](/dictation-output/).

<Figure src="/images/blog/how-to-use-letterly-keyboard-6.webp" alt="The rewrite options menu" caption="The rewrite options menu" />

## Keyboard menu

Tap the menu button on the left of the keyboard.

<Figure src="/images/blog/how-to-use-letterly-keyboard-7.webp" alt="Actions in the keyboard menu" caption="Actions in the keyboard menu" />

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Undo** | Removes the text Letterly inserted, or reverts a voice rewrite |
| **Redo** | Restores what Undo removed |
| **Add to Dictionary** | Saves a word so Letterly spells it correctly next time. See [Dictionary](/dictionary/) |
| **History** | Opens your dictation History in Letterly |

## Turn Dictation off

Open the Letterly Home screen and tap the **power icon** next to **Dictation** to turn it off and disable keyboard access. You can also tap the power icon in the Letterly widget on the Lock Screen, without unlocking your iPhone.

<Callout type="note" title="How do I use Letterly with other apps on my iPhone?">

Add the Letterly Keyboard once, and it is available in every app that has a text field — Messages, Mail, Notes, your browser, anywhere you type.
