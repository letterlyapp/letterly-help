**Record more** adds a new recording to an existing note instead of creating a new one.

<Callout type="note">

**Record more** is available on Pro. On the Free plan you will see it in the note with a **Pro** label.

<MoreLink href="/what-plans-are-there/">Learn more about plans →</MoreLink>

</Callout>

## To record more:

<Steps>

1. Open the note and tap the **⋯** menu.
2. Choose **Record more**.
3. Speak, then tap or click **Stop**.

</Steps>

<Video src="/images/help-center/record-more.mp4" caption="Recording more into an existing note" />

Letterly transcribes the new part and adds it to the same note.

<Callout type="note">

You can leave the note while the new part is being transcribed. Letterly finishes it in the background.

</Callout>

Each Record more session is stored as a separate recording, so you can play back any part of the note. See [Listen to the audio](/listen-to-the-audio/).
