Not directly. Letterly records through the microphone, so it captures what the microphone hears — not the audio stream of the call.

To record a meeting, put your phone nearby with Letterly recording. The phone picks up both sides of the conversation, like a voice recorder. No bots, no meeting links, no setup.

<Callout type="warn">

With headphones on, only your own voice is recorded. Use speakers so the other participants can be heard.

</Callout>
