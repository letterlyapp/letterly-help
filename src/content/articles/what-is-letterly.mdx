Letterly turns speech into clean, ready-to-use text — whether it's your own voice or someone else's. You record, and you get text you can actually use: a message, a note, a summary.

It works in three ways:

- **Dictate in any app** — speak instead of typing in Gmail, Slack, Messages, forms. See [What is the Dictation mode?](/what-is-the-dictation-mode/)
- **Capture your own thoughts** — notes, journaling, posts, longer writing you come back to. See [What is the “Record myself” mode?](/what-is-the-record-myself-mode/)
- **Record talks** — meetings, lectures, interviews — and get a transcript split by speaker plus a recap. See [What is the Meeting mode?](/what-is-the-meeting-mode/)
