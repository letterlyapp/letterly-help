Your recordings and notes travel between your device and our servers over a secure, encrypted connection. They are stored on Google Cloud, enterprise-grade infrastructure professionally managed by Google.

## How long we keep it

We keep your information only as long as it is needed for the purposes described in our [Privacy Policy](https://letterly.app/policy/) — normally, for as long as you have an account with us — unless a longer period is required by law.

When there is no longer a legitimate reason to process your information, we delete or anonymize it. If that is not possible right away — for example, because a copy sits in a backup archive — we store it securely and isolate it from any further processing until deletion is possible.

We use appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data.
