<PlatformTabs>
<Platform name="iphone">

Letterly is a free download from the App Store.

## Option 1 — Search the App Store

<Steps>

1. Open the **App Store** on your iPhone.
2. Tap **Search** and type **Letterly**.
3. Check that the icon and name match the listing, then tap **Get** to install.

</Steps>

## Option 2 — Open a direct link

On your iPhone, tap [this App Store link](https://apps.apple.com/app/letterly-voice-to-clear-text/id6464049772) to jump straight to the listing.

Once it is installed, tap **Open** — you go straight into [Onboarding](/onboarding/).

</Platform>
<Platform name="android">

Letterly is a free download from Google Play.

## Option 1 — Search Google Play

<Steps>

1. Open **Google Play** on your Android phone.
2. Search **Letterly**.
3. Check that it matches the listing, then tap **Install**.

</Steps>

## Option 2 — Open a direct link

Tap [this Google Play link](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.draftai) on your phone.

Once it is installed, tap **Open** to begin [Onboarding](/onboarding/).

</Platform>
<Platform name="mac">

Letterly for Mac is a free download from the Mac App Store.

## Option 1 — Search the Mac App Store

<Steps>

1. Open the **App Store** on your Mac.
2. Search **Letterly** and check that it matches the listing.
3. Click **Get**, then **Install**.

</Steps>

## Option 2 — Open a direct link

Open [Letterly on the Mac App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/letterly-ai-voice-note-taking/id6464049772) to jump straight to the listing.

Once it is installed, click **Open** — you go straight into [Onboarding](/onboarding/).

<Callout type="warn">

There is no separate download outside the Mac App Store. If a site offers a “.dmg” of Letterly, it is not from us.

</Callout>

</Platform>
<Platform name="windows">

Letterly for Windows is a free download from the Microsoft Store.

## Option 1 — Search the Microsoft Store

<Steps>

1. Open the **Microsoft Store** on your PC.
2. Search **Letterly** and check that it matches the listing.
3. Click **Get** to install.

</Steps>

## Option 2 — Open a direct link

Open [Letterly in the Microsoft Store](https://apps.microsoft.com/detail/9n0fkgsz4x60) to jump straight to the listing.

</Platform>
<Platform name="web">

There is nothing to install on the web.

## Open Letterly in your browser

<Steps>

1. Go to [web.letterly.app](https://web.letterly.app).
2. Sign in with the same email you use on your other devices.
3. Allow microphone access when the browser asks.

</Steps>
