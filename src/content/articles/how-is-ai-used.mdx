Letterly is an AI tool because it uses AI models for two jobs: turning speech into text, and reshaping that text for you.

## Transcription

When you record, an AI speech model converts your audio into accurate text — across 90+ languages, detected automatically. See [What languages are supported?](/what-languages-are-supported/)

## Rewriting

Once you have a transcript, a language model can **rewrite** it into the format you need — a tidy email, a summary, a post, bullet points — while keeping your meaning. You choose the style. See [Rewrite options](/rewrite-options/).

<Callout type="note">

Your recordings are handled securely and are **not used to train AI**.
