Favorite rewrite options are pinned to the **top** of your list so you can reach them quickly — including while dictating.

<Callout type="note">

Favorites are available on **Pro**. On the Free plan you have the **Magic** option.

<MoreLink href="/what-plans-are-there/">Learn more about plans →</MoreLink>

</Callout>

Tap the **star** next to an option to add it to your favorites. Tap the star again to remove it — the option stays in your list.
