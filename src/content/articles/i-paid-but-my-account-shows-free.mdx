In most cases the subscription is fine — the app is simply signed in with a different email than the one used for the purchase.

<Steps>

1. Open **Settings → Letterly Account** and look at the email shown.
2. Check that it is the **same email you used when you purchased**.
3. If it is different, log out and sign in again with the purchase email.

</Steps>

<Callout type="tip">

If the email matches and the plan is still Free, write to [hi@letterly.app](mailto:hi@letterly.app) — we will fix it on our side.

</Callout>
