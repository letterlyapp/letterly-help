Where you manage or cancel your subscription depends on **where you bought it**.

## Bought on our website

<Steps>

1. Open the [subscription portal](https://billing.stripe.com/p/login/7sIbKLg7HfaKe4M9AA).
2. Enter the **email you used for the purchase** and request a login link.
3. Manage or cancel your subscription there.

</Steps>

<Callout type="warn">

Use the purchase email. With any other address the portal will not find your subscription.

</Callout>

## Bought in the App Store

Open **Settings → your name → Subscriptions** on your iPhone or iPad, choose **Letterly**, and cancel there. Apple manages these subscriptions, so we cannot cancel them for you.

## Bought in Google Play

Open the **Google Play** app → **Payments and subscriptions → Subscriptions**, choose **Letterly**, and cancel there.

<Callout type="tip">

Cannot find your subscription anywhere? Write to [hi@letterly.app](mailto:hi@letterly.app) and we will locate it.

</Callout>

Cancelling stops future charges. You keep Pro until the end of the period you already paid for — see [What happens when my subscription ends?](/what-happens-when-my-subscription-ends/)

## Change the email your subscription is linked to

Your subscription is tied to the email you used at purchase. To move it to another address, write to [hi@letterly.app](mailto:hi@letterly.app) from the **current** email and tell us the new one.

<Callout type="note">

We do this manually so that nobody else can claim your subscription.

</Callout>
