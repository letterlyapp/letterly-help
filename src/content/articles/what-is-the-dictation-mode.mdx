<PlatformTabs>
<Platform name="iphone">

**Dictation** lets you speak instead of typing, directly in the apps you already use. Letterly inserts clean, ready-to-use text at your cursor.

<Callout type="note">

On the **Free** plan: **20 dictations**, each up to **1 minute**.

<MoreLink href="/what-plans-are-there/">Learn more about plans →</MoreLink>

</Callout>

Use it in Messages, WhatsApp, Mail, Gmail, Notes, Slack, ChatGPT, search fields, forms, and almost anywhere else you can type.

## How it works

<Steps>

1. Tap a text field so the cursor is in it.
2. Switch to the [Letterly Keyboard](/keyboard/) with the globe icon and tap the **microphone**.
3. Say what you want to write.
4. Tap **Stop**. Letterly inserts the finished text at the cursor.

</Steps>

<Callout type="note">

A single dictation can be up to **15 minutes**. Need longer? Record a note in the app instead — up to 90 minutes.

</Callout>

<Callout type="tip">

To get started, follow the [Set up Dictation](/set-up-dictation/) guide.

</Callout>
