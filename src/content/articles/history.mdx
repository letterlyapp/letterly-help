<Platform name="iphone">

Letterly saves every completed dictation in **History**. Use it to find text that was not inserted, or to reuse something you dictated earlier.

<Callout type="note">

Dictation History stays on the device where it was created and does not sync to the cloud or your other devices.

<MoreLink href="/history/">Learn more about Dictation History →</MoreLink>

</Callout>

## Open History

You can open History in two places:

- In the Letterly Keyboard, tap the menu button, then tap **History**.
- In the Letterly app, open **Settings → History**.

Entries are grouped by date, most recent first.

<Shot label="Screenshot: dictation History on iPhone" />

## Actions

Press and hold an entry, or swipe left on it.

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Copy** | Copies the text to your clipboard |
| **Export audio** | Saves or shares the original recording as an audio file |
| **Delete** | Removes the dictation from History |

<Callout type="warn">

Deleted dictations cannot be restored. Letterly asks you to confirm before deleting.

</Callout>

</Platform>

## Open History

Open **Settings → Dictation → History**. Entries are grouped by date, most recent first.

<Shot label="Screenshot: dictation History on Mac" />

## Actions

Right-click an entry, or click and hold it.

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Copy** | Copies the text to your clipboard |
| **Delete** | Removes the entry from History |

<Callout type="note">

Your latest dictation is also copied to the clipboard automatically. If the text was not inserted, place your cursor and press **Command + V**.

</Callout>
