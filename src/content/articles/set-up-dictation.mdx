<Platform name="iphone">

On iPhone you dictate with the **Letterly Keyboard**. Add it once, and you can dictate in any app without opening Letterly.

<Callout type="note">

Update Letterly to the latest version before you start.

</Callout>

Open the app and tap **Set up** on the **Letterly Dictation** banner on the Home screen. If you tapped **Later** before, open **Settings → Dictation** and tap **Set up** there.

<Figure src="/images/blog/how-to-use-letterly-keyboard-1.webp" alt="The Letterly Dictation banner on the Home screen" caption="The Letterly Dictation banner on the Home screen" />

<Figure src="/images/blog/how-to-use-letterly-keyboard-2.webp" alt="Or start the setup from Settings → Dictation" caption="Or start the setup from Settings → Dictation" />

## Step 1. Add the Letterly Keyboard

<Steps>

1. In Letterly, tap **Open Settings**.
2. In your iPhone settings, tap **Keyboards**.
3. Turn on **Letterly**.

</Steps>

<Callout type="tip">

New to Letterly? If you are prompted, allow microphone access before you start dictating.

</Callout>

<Figure src="/images/blog/how-to-use-letterly-keyboard-3.webp" alt="Add the Letterly Keyboard in iPhone settings" caption="Add the Letterly Keyboard in iPhone settings" />

## Step 2. Enable Full Access

After you turn on the **Letterly Keyboard**, the **Allow Full Access** option appears below it on the same screen. Turn it on.

### Why Letterly needs Full Access

Full Access is **a technical requirement** that allows the Letterly Keyboard to process your dictation and return the finished text to the keyboard. **Without this permission, Dictation cannot work.**

Apple displays a standard warning for every third-party keyboard that uses Full Access. The warning describes what this permission technically allows — it does not describe how Letterly handles your data.

<Callout type="safe">

Letterly does not misuse, sell, or share your data. Your Dictation History is stored locally on your device, and no one on the Letterly team has direct access to your personal notes or dictated content.

</Callout>

<Figure src="/images/blog/how-to-use-letterly-keyboard-4.webp" alt="Enable Full Access for the Letterly Keyboard" caption="Enable Full Access for the Letterly Keyboard" />

## Step 3. Return to Letterly

After you turn on **Allow Full Access**, tap **Letterly** in the top-left corner to return to the app and complete the setup.

You can now dictate in Messages, Mail, Notes, WhatsApp, ChatGPT, and other apps.

<Figure src="/images/blog/how-to-use-letterly-keyboard-4-1.webp" alt="Tap Letterly in the top-left corner to return to the app" caption="Tap Letterly in the top-left corner to return to the app" />

</Platform>
<Platform name="android">

On Android you dictate with the **Dictation Bubble** — a floating button that stays on top of your other apps.

<Callout type="note">

Update Letterly to the latest version before you start.

</Callout>

Open the app and tap **Set up** on the **Letterly Dictation** banner on the Home screen. If you tapped **Later** before, open **Settings → Dictation** and tap **Set up** there.

You will grant two permissions:

<Steps>

1. **Display over other apps**, so the Bubble can appear while you use other apps.
2. **Accessibility**, so Letterly can detect the active text field and insert your dictated text there.

</Steps>

## Step 1. Allow the Bubble to appear over other apps

On the setup screen, tap **Continue** next to **Show the Dictation Bubble**, then tap **Open Settings**.

<Figure src="/images/blog/how-to-use-dictation-bubble-on-android-1.webp" alt="Tap Continue, then Open Settings" caption="Tap Continue, then Open Settings" />

Android opens the **Display over other apps** settings. Depending on your device, you may first see a list of apps.

<Steps>

1. Find and tap **Letterly**.
2. Turn on **Allow display over other apps**.

</Steps>

<Figure src="/images/blog/how-to-use-dictation-bubble-on-android-2.webp" alt="Turn on Allow display over other apps for Letterly" caption="Turn on Allow display over other apps for Letterly" />

<Callout type="note" title="Why Letterly needs this permission">

Android calls it **Display over other apps**. It allows the floating Dictation Bubble to appear above WhatsApp, Gmail, Messages, ChatGPT and other apps. Without it, the Bubble cannot appear outside Letterly.

</Callout>

After enabling the permission, return to Letterly to continue the setup.

## Step 2. Allow Letterly to insert text

The second permission lets Letterly find the text field where you are writing and insert your finished dictation there.

Tap **Open Settings** next to **Insert text in any app**. Before opening your Android settings, Letterly explains how it uses Accessibility Services — review it and tap **Continue**.

<Callout type="safe" title="Why Letterly uses Accessibility Services">

Letterly uses Android's AccessibilityService API only to detect the active text field and insert your dictated text into it. Letterly does **not** use this permission to read passwords, payment details or banking fields, record calls, change system settings, or control your device without your action.

</Callout>

<Figure src="/images/blog/how-to-use-dictation-bubble-on-android-3.webp" alt="Review how Letterly uses Accessibility, then tap Continue" caption="Review how Letterly uses Accessibility, then tap Continue" />

Android opens the **Accessibility** settings. Menu names vary by device — on many phones the path is:

<Steps>

1. Tap **Downloaded apps**.
2. Tap **Letterly**.
3. Turn on **Use Letterly**.

</Steps>

<Figure src="/images/blog/how-to-use-dictation-bubble-on-android-4.webp" alt="Turn on Use Letterly under Accessibility" caption="Turn on Use Letterly under Accessibility" />

## Step 3. Confirm the Android warning

Some Android devices show an additional system warning, because Accessibility is considered a sensitive permission. Android shows it for any app that uses Accessibility Services.

<Callout type="safe">

This is a **standard system notice**. It does not describe Letterly's privacy practices. Letterly uses the permission only to detect the active text field and insert your dictated text — we do not use sensitive information for anything else, sell it, share it with third parties for their own use, or use it to train AI models. The Letterly team has no direct access to your notes or dictated content.

</Callout>

<Steps>

1. Wait for the countdown to finish, if one appears.
2. Select **I'm aware of the possible risks**.
3. Tap **OK**.

</Steps>

<Figure src="/images/blog/how-to-use-dictation-bubble-on-android-5.webp" alt="Confirm the Android Accessibility warning" caption="Confirm the Android Accessibility warning" />

Return to Letterly. The setup is complete and the Dictation Bubble appears on your screen — press and hold it to drag it anywhere you like.

</Platform>
<Platform name="mac">

On Mac you dictate with a **shortcut** or by clicking the **floating bar**. Setup takes two permissions.

## Step 1. Open Letterly on your Mac

Get the app from the [Mac App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/letterly-ai-voice-note-taking/id6464049772), open it, and go to **Settings**.

<Figure src="/images/blog/how-to-set-up-letterly-dictation-on-mac-1.webp" alt="Open Letterly on your Mac and go to Settings" caption="Open Letterly on your Mac and go to Settings" />

## Step 2. Find Dictation

In Settings, open the **Dictation** section.

<Figure src="/images/blog/how-to-set-up-letterly-dictation-on-mac-2.webp" alt="The Dictation section in Settings" caption="The Dictation section in Settings" />

## Step 3. Allow the required permissions

Letterly guides you through two permissions: **Microphone access**, so it can hear what you dictate, and **Accessibility access**, so it can insert the finished text into the active text field.

### Microphone access

<Callout type="note">

If you have used Letterly before, microphone access may already be enabled — this step is skipped automatically.

</Callout>

<Steps>

1. Click **Allow** in Letterly.
2. When the **No microphone access** message appears, click **Settings**.
3. Enable Letterly under **Microphone**.
4. Return to Letterly to continue the setup.

</Steps>

<Figure src="/images/blog/how-to-set-up-letterly-dictation-on-mac-perm-1.webp" alt="Allow microphone access for Letterly" caption="Allow microphone access for Letterly" />

<Figure src="/images/blog/how-to-set-up-letterly-dictation-on-mac-perm-2.webp" alt="Open Settings to enable microphone access" caption="Open Settings to enable microphone access" />

<Figure src="/images/blog/how-to-set-up-letterly-dictation-on-mac-perm-3.webp" alt="Enable Letterly under Microphone" caption="Enable Letterly under Microphone" />

### Accessibility access

<Steps>

1. Click **Allow** in Letterly.
2. Enable Letterly under **Accessibility**.
3. If macOS asks you to, quit and reopen Letterly.

</Steps>

<Figure src="/images/blog/how-to-set-up-letterly-dictation-on-mac-perm-4.webp" alt="Allow Accessibility access for Letterly" caption="Allow Accessibility access for Letterly" />

<Figure src="/images/blog/how-to-set-up-letterly-dictation-on-mac-perm-5.webp" alt="Enable Letterly under Accessibility" caption="Enable Letterly under Accessibility" />

<Figure src="/images/blog/how-to-set-up-letterly-dictation-on-mac-perm-6.webp" alt="Quit and reopen Letterly to finish the setup" caption="Quit and reopen Letterly to finish the setup" />

</Platform>
<Platform name="windows">

On Windows you dictate with a **shortcut** or by clicking the **floating bar**.

## Step 1. Install Letterly

Get the app from the [Microsoft Store](https://apps.microsoft.com/detail/9n0fkgsz4x60) and open it. Full details: [Download & install](/download-and-install/).

## Step 2. Sign in

Letterly on Windows works only with an account, so signing in is the first thing that happens. The app opens the sign-in page in your web browser.

<Steps>

1. Choose **Continue with Google**, **Continue with Apple**, or **Continue with Email**.
2. On the **Open the Letterly desktop app** screen, check the account and click **Continue**.
3. The desktop app opens on its own and takes you to the Dictation setup.

</Steps>

<Figure src="/images/blog/how-to-set-up-letterly-dictation-on-windows-2.webp" alt="Sign in in your browser, then continue to the desktop app" caption="Sign in in your browser, then continue to the desktop app" />

<Callout type="note">

If you already have a subscription, sign in with the email you bought it with. Otherwise you start on the Free plan.

<MoreLink href="/what-plans-are-there/">Learn more about plans →</MoreLink>

</Callout>

## Step 3. Open Dictation

After signing in, the app takes you straight to the Dictation setup. If you closed it, open **Settings → Dictation**.

<Figure src="/images/blog/how-to-set-up-letterly-dictation-on-windows-3.webp" alt="Open Settings and go to the Dictation section" caption="Open Settings and go to the Dictation section" />

## Step 4. Allow microphone access

<Callout type="note">

If you have used Letterly before, microphone access may already be enabled — this step is skipped automatically.

</Callout>

<Steps>

1. Click **Allow** in Letterly.
2. When the Windows permission window appears, click **Yes**.

</Steps>

<Figure src="/images/blog/how-to-set-up-letterly-dictation-on-windows-4.webp" alt="Allow microphone access for Letterly on Windows" caption="Allow microphone access for Letterly on Windows" />

## Step 5. Choose your floating bar setting

The floating bar is a small on-screen control you can click to start recording at any time, even when Letterly is not in focus.

<Steps>

1. Turn on **Always show** if you want it visible at all times.
2. Click **Continue**.

</Steps>

<Callout type="tip">

You can change this later in **Settings → Dictation → Floating bar**.

</Callout>

<Figure src="/images/blog/how-to-set-up-letterly-dictation-on-windows-5.webp" alt="Choose whether the floating bar always stays visible" caption="Choose whether the floating bar always stays visible" />

## Step 6. Check your shortcut

Your default shortcut is **Ctrl + Win**. It works two ways: **hold to talk** for something short, or **press once to start and again to stop**. Click **Continue**.

<Callout type="tip">

Change it any time in **Settings → Dictation → Floating bar → Shortcut**, or in **Settings → Shortcuts → Dictation**. See [Shortcuts](/shortcuts/).

</Callout>

<Figure src="/images/blog/how-to-set-up-letterly-dictation-on-windows-6.webp" alt="The default Ctrl + Win shortcut" caption="The default Ctrl + Win shortcut" />
