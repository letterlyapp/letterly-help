A few things to check:

- Make sure dictation is set up for your device — see [Set up Dictation](/set-up-dictation/).
- Make sure you granted the access Letterly asked for during setup.
- Place your cursor in an editable text field before you start.

Still not inserting? Email [hi@letterly.app](mailto:hi@letterly.app) with your device and app version.
