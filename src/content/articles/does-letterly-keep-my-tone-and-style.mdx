Yes. Keeping your voice is the point of Letterly.

The transcript is your own words, exactly as you said them. Letterly only makes them readable: it removes filler words like “um” and “ehm”, adds punctuation, and fixes obvious slips of the tongue. Nothing is rephrased or invented.

How much changes after that is up to you, and depends on the rewrite option you choose:

| Rewrite option | How much it changes |
| --- | --- |
| **Raw Transcript** | Nothing — your words exactly as spoken |
| **Slightly** | Tidies up the text, keeps your wording and tone |
| **Magic** | Cleans up rough thoughts into a clear, readable text |
| **Significantly** | Fully reshapes the text into a new structure |

<Callout type="tip">

Want your text polished but still sounding like you? Choose **Slightly**.

</Callout>

You can also create your own rewrite option with instructions in your own words — for example, “keep my wording, only fix grammar”. See [Custom rewrite options](/custom-rewrite-options/).
