**Recording works offline** — and, most importantly, your recording is not lost if you have no connection.

Transcription and rewriting need the internet, because they run on AI models in the cloud. If you record offline, Letterly saves your audio and finishes transcribing and rewriting **automatically as soon as you are back online**.

<Callout type="note">

Transcription turns your voice into text, and rewriting turns that text into the version you need.
